Getting help

Where to ask a question, where to report a problem, and how to get an answer quickly.

Before you ask

  • Check Troubleshooting and the FAQ.
  • If the problem started after an update, going back to a restore point from before it usually fixes it straight away. See Going back.

Where to ask

ForGo to
Questions, advice, and problems you are not sure are bugsr/SakuraOS on Reddit
Bugs in SakuraOS: the installer, App Store, Update Center, settings page, Terminal Assist or recoveryGitHub issues
A mistake in this wikiGitHub issues
A security problemNot in public. See the Security policy.
News and announcementsThe blog and @ThesakuraOS on X

Ask about SakuraOS problems in the SakuraOS community rather than in Arch Linux's forums. SakuraOS sets up parts of the system differently from Arch, and Arch's community supports Arch only.

What to include

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Whether it started after an update, and roughly when.
  • Your hardware, if it could be related: see Logs and bug reports.
  • Any error message, copied as text rather than as a photo where possible.

For bugs, attach the relevant log; Logs and bug reports shows how to get it.
